About BloodConnect

BloodConnect is a blood banking system that brings donors, patients, hospitals and blood banks onto one page, so that blood reaches the person who needs it in time.

Why we exist

Blood cannot be manufactured. It can only come from donors, and it has a short shelf life: red cells last about 42 days and platelets only about 5. Hospitals often need a specific blood group at short notice, and families end up calling around, posting on social media and hoping the message reaches the right person. BloodConnect replaces that scramble with one place where donors register, patients ask, and verified hospitals respond.

What you can do here

Donors

Register with your blood group and contact details. Your donation is added to the available stock straight away, and hospitals can reach you when your group is needed. You can also find upcoming donation camps near you.

Patients and families

Use Want Blood to say which group you need and by when. Every approved hospital and blood bank is emailed your details. If the blood is needed within 24 hours, your request is also posted on the homepage as an emergency.

Hospitals and blood banks

Register your institution and, once the admin approves it, receive blood requests by email and organise donation camps. Each hospital can manage only its own camps.

Admin

The admin approves hospital registrations and keeps the site trustworthy by reviewing donors, requests and camps, and removing anything that should not be there.

How a request works

1. Request

The patient or a family member fills in the Want Blood form with the blood group, contact details and a deadline.

2. Alert

All approved hospitals and blood banks receive an email with the patient's details.

3. Emergency

If the deadline is under 24 hours, the request appears on the homepage with an emergency banner so donors can respond directly.

4. Contact

A hospital or donor calls the patient on the number given and arranges the donation.

Who can donate?

Rules differ between countries and blood centres, and the doctor at the donation site always makes the final decision. As a general guide, a donor:

  • is between 18 and 65 years old (this site accepts registrations in that range)
  • weighs at least 45 kg and feels healthy on the day
  • has a haemoglobin level in the accepted range, which is checked before donating
  • has not donated whole blood in the last 3 to 4 months
  • is not ill, pregnant, or taking medicines that rule out donation

What happens when you donate

A short registration and health check comes first: your pulse, blood pressure and haemoglobin are measured. The donation itself takes about 10 to 15 minutes and collects roughly 350 to 450 ml of blood. You then rest, have a drink and a snack, and most people carry on with their day. One donation can be separated into red cells, plasma and platelets, and can help up to three patients.

Privacy and trust

  • Only an admin-approved hospital can log in, post camps or read blood requests.
  • Donor details are visible only to the admin. The public sees just the number of donors.
  • Emergency requests show the patient's name, address and mobile number on the homepage so that donors can respond quickly. Only use the Want Blood form if you are comfortable with this.
  • Hospitals can delete only their own camps. The admin can remove any entry that is wrong or misused.

Common questions

Do donors or patients need an account?

No. Only hospitals and blood banks register and log in. Donors and patients simply fill in a form.

Is BloodConnect a hospital or a blood bank?

No. BloodConnect is a connecting platform. Blood is collected, tested and stored by licensed hospitals and blood banks, and any medical decision is made by their doctors.

Does a registration guarantee that blood is available?

The availability figures are based on donors registered on this site and are a guide only. Always confirm stock with the hospital or blood bank before relying on it.

My hospital registered but cannot log in.

New hospital accounts must be approved by the admin first. You can log in as soon as the approval is done.

Is the service free?

Yes. BloodConnect is free for donors, patients and hospitals, and blood should never be sold through this site.
